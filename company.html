About Quantera

We build engineering tools that find quantum-vulnerable cryptography before it becomes a breach — deployed privately, for security teams who can't send source code to a third party.

01 — Mission

"Migrate to post-quantum cryptography" is easy to say and hard to do at the scale of a real codebase. Quantera exists to make it an engineering problem, not a guessing game — real static analysis of your code, running inside your own environment, so finding and fixing quantum-vulnerable cryptography doesn't mean handing your source code to someone else.

02 — Values

How We Work

The principles behind every deployment.

Privacy by Design

Your data stays inside your environment by default, not as an afterthought.

Precision

Findings are grounded in your own code — every recommendation traces to a file, a line, and how that key is actually used, not a generic lookup table.

Partnership

We work alongside your security and IT teams from day one, not after deployment.

Continuous Trust

Migration guidance evolves as NIST standards, your codebase, and your organization's compliance needs do.
