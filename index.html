02 — Impact

Projected Impact & ROI

A model of the time and cost impact private AI can have on a typical 100-person enterprise team.

Not measured customer results — modeled from stated assumptions: a traditional workflow of ~30 min/day/employee searching for information plus ~15 min/day resolving duplicated work, reduced to ~5 minutes each with instant retrieval, at an illustrative $50/hour fully-loaded labor cost and 250 working days/year. Change the assumptions and the numbers change; actual results depend on your organization's workflows.

Under the stated assumptions, cutting routine search and rework time saves a modeled 14,600+ hours annually for a 100-person team — roughly 3.5 work weeks reclaimed per employee.
