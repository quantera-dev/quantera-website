Platform

How Quantera Works

A private, local-first architecture built to keep your data inside your own environment at every step.

01 — Pipeline

From Your Data to an Answer

Four steps, all running inside your own environment.

Ingest

Connect your existing document stores and internal knowledge sources.

Index

Content is processed and indexed entirely within your private environment.

Retrieve

Natural-language queries surface the most relevant internal content in real time.

Answer

Grounded, context-aware answers are generated from your own data — nothing external, nothing exposed.

02 — Architecture

Security by Design

The same architecture principles that make Quantera private also make it secure.

End-to-End Encryption

All communications are encrypted in transit and at rest, ensuring data integrity at every step.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions ensure users only access what they need, maintaining strict data governance.

Local-First Processing

Data stays within your environment. No external APIs, no cloud dependencies, complete control.

Compliance Ready

Built to support GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and other regulatory frameworks critical to your industry.

03 — Integrations

Works With What You Already Have

Quantera connects to your existing document systems and internal knowledge stores rather than asking you to migrate everything into a new one. Your workflows stay the same — what changes is how quickly you can find and use what's already there.
