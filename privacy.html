Privacy Policy

Quantera AI — Meta/Facebook and Instagram App

Effective Date: August 9, 2026  •  Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Quantera AI's website at quanteraai.io and to any Quantera AI application that allows users to connect Meta services, including Facebook and Instagram, for content creation, approval, scheduling, publishing, account management, or analytics. It also applies to information we receive through Meta APIs when you authorize our application.

This Policy does not replace Meta's own privacy policies or terms. Your use of Facebook, Instagram, and other Meta products remains subject to the policies and terms provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Depending on how you use Quantera AI, we may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, company name, job title, and other contact information.
  • Brand information, campaign goals, content preferences, approved messaging, prompts, drafts, and other materials you submit.
  • Content you upload or attach, such as images, videos, documents, logos, and marketing assets.
  • Scheduling preferences, approval decisions, publishing instructions, and account settings.
  • Communications you send to us, including support, privacy, or data deletion requests.

2.2 Information Received from Meta

If you connect a Facebook or Instagram account, we may receive Meta Platform Data only to the extent permitted by the permissions you grant and the Meta APIs enabled for the application. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Basic account and profile information, such as account identifiers, username, profile name, profile picture, or account type.
  • Facebook Page or Instagram Professional Account identifiers and related account metadata.
  • Information about Pages or professional accounts you are authorized to manage, where required to enable publishing features.
  • Media information, including post identifiers, captions, media URLs or references, timestamps, and publication status.
  • Permissions and authorization information needed to connect your account and perform actions you approve.
  • Publishing-related data, including the content you direct Quantera AI to publish and the result or status of the publishing request.
  • Insights or engagement metrics, if you authorize analytics permissions, such as impressions, reach, interactions, clicks, or similar performance data made available by Meta.
  • OAuth access tokens, refresh-related information, or equivalent credentials required to maintain an authorized connection.

Quantera AI does not receive every category listed above in every case. The specific information available to us depends on the Meta permissions you approve, your account type, the features you enable, and Meta's current API capabilities.

2.3 Technical and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, application events, error logs, security events, and feature usage. Our website may also use cookies or basic analytics technologies to understand traffic and improve reliability.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information covered by this Policy to:

  • Authenticate users and connect authorized Facebook or Instagram accounts.
  • Generate platform-specific social media drafts, captions, hashtags, and related content.
  • Present content for review, editing, approval, or rejection before publication.
  • Schedule and publish content only after the applicable approval or automated rule has been satisfied.
  • Retrieve publishing status and, where authorized, engagement or performance metrics.
  • Maintain campaign history, content calendars, audit records, and user-selected preferences.
  • Improve the quality, reliability, security, and usability of the Quantera AI service.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, security incidents, or violations of our terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. AI-Assisted Content Generation

Quantera AI may use artificial intelligence to help create, rewrite, summarize, format, or optimize social-media content. Information you submit for content generation may be processed by Quantera AI systems or by service providers acting on our behalf, depending on the configuration of the application.

We do not use Meta Platform Data to train a generalized or publicly available AI model. If engagement or performance information is used to improve future content recommendations, it is used to provide or improve functionality for the applicable Quantera AI user or account, subject to the permissions and settings enabled by that user.

5. Publishing and User Direction

Quantera AI is designed as an approval-first publishing system. Unless you specifically enable an automated publishing rule, content is intended to remain in draft or pending-approval status until an authorized user approves it.

When you approve, schedule, or otherwise authorize a post, you instruct Quantera AI to transmit the approved content to the applicable social-media platform through its API. You are responsible for reviewing content before publication and for ensuring that content complies with applicable laws, platform rules, and your organization's policies.

6. How We Share Information

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • Meta and its services, when necessary to authenticate your account, publish content, retrieve permitted account information, or obtain authorized analytics.
  • Hosting, database, security, communications, analytics, and infrastructure providers that process information on our behalf.
  • AI or software service providers used to provide requested content-generation or application functionality, where applicable.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, or legal counsel when reasonably necessary.
  • Government authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the service.
  • A successor or acquiring entity in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable legal protections.

We do not sell Meta Platform Data or personal information obtained through Meta for monetary consideration. We do not use Meta Platform Data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. OAuth Credentials and Access Tokens

Quantera AI may store OAuth access tokens or similar authorization credentials so that the application can perform the actions you authorize. These credentials are treated as sensitive information, are intended to be stored server-side using secure secret-management or encrypted storage, and are not intentionally exposed in the public frontend or application source code.

You may revoke Quantera AI's access through the relevant Facebook or Instagram account settings or by contacting us. Revoking access may disable connected publishing, analytics, or account-management functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business and security records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

  • OAuth credentials are retained only while needed to maintain an authorized connection or until they expire, are revoked, or are deleted.
  • Drafts, scheduled posts, publishing records, campaign data, and analytics may be retained while your account is active or as needed to provide requested functionality.
  • Security and audit logs may be retained for a reasonable period to investigate abuse, troubleshoot failures, and demonstrate system integrity.
  • When deletion is requested, we will delete or de-identify covered information as required, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup-retention exceptions.

9. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. These measures may include HTTPS/TLS, access controls, role-based permissions, encryption where appropriate, secure credential storage, logging, and restricted administrative access.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about how your data is used. You may also have the right to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing.

To exercise a privacy right, contact admin@quanteraai.io. We may need to verify your identity or authority before completing a request.

11. Meta/Facebook User Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Quantera AI's Facebook or Instagram integration at any time.

To request deletion:

  1. Email admin@quanteraai.io with the subject line "Meta Data Deletion Request."
  2. Identify the Facebook or Instagram account connected to Quantera AI. Do not send passwords or access tokens.
  3. Specify that you want Quantera AI to delete data received from or associated with your Meta-connected account.

After receiving and verifying a valid request, Quantera AI will initiate deletion promptly and will delete or de-identify applicable Meta-derived data from active systems, except where retention is required by law, necessary for security or fraud prevention, or temporarily maintained in protected backups. Where technically applicable, deauthorizing or disconnecting Quantera AI from Meta may also trigger deletion procedures.

Quantera AI may also provide a dedicated data-deletion URL or callback endpoint for Meta's automated user-data deletion process. If such an endpoint is configured, deletion requests received through that mechanism will be processed in accordance with this section. See our Data Deletion page for full instructions.

12. Cookies and Website Analytics

Quantera AI's website may use cookies or basic analytics tools to operate the site, remember preferences, measure traffic, diagnose errors, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

13. Children's Privacy

Quantera AI is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the service. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

14. International Data Transfers

If information is processed outside the country in which you are located, we will handle such transfers in accordance with applicable law and use appropriate contractual, technical, or organizational safeguards where required.

15. Third-Party Services

Quantera AI may integrate with third-party platforms and services, including Meta products. Those third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Quantera AI is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our application, integrations, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised version with an updated "Last Updated" date. If a change materially affects how we handle personal information, we may provide additional notice where required.
