Federal Software Contractors

Turn PQC requirements into actionable software migration.

Crypto inventory, migration plans, evidence, and standards mapping — for suppliers preparing for government and customer PQC requirements.

The Pain

What makes this hard for contractors specifically

Hundreds of repositories

Cryptography is scattered across product lines, some maintained for a decade or more.

Crypto inventory requests

Agencies and primes are starting to ask for a cryptographic bill of materials you may not have.

Future contracting requirements

PQC readiness is moving from "nice to have" toward a contracting expectation.

Limited cryptography specialists

Most engineering teams don't have a cryptographer on staff to triage findings.

What You Need

Inventory, plans, evidence, standards mapping

Quantera builds a repository-level cryptographic inventory, maps findings to NIST-standardized recommendations (ML-KEM/FIPS 203, ML-DSA/FIPS 204), and produces the verification evidence a reviewer or prime contractor can actually read.
